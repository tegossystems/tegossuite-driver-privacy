TegosSuite Driver

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how personal information is used when you use the TegosSuite Driver Android app (the “App”). The App is provided for drivers carrying out work for an employer or business customer.

Application provider: Tegos Systems Ltd

Postal address: 18 Lower Byrom Street, Manchester, M3 4AP, United Kingdom

Contact email: info@tegos.eu

Who is responsible for your information?

Tegos Systems Ltd provides TegosSuite Driver to business customers.

The organisation that provides your driver account, usually your employer or contracting organisation, is generally the data controller for your operational information, including tours, jobs and vehicle checks.

Tegos Systems Ltd acts as a data processor on behalf of its business customers. Tegos processes personal information to provide, maintain, support and secure TegosSuite Driver in accordance with its customers' instructions.

Driver accounts and work assignments are managed through the customer's Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central system. The customer organisation is responsible for determining and informing drivers of the lawful basis for processing operational information.

For questions about your operational information or to exercise your data protection rights, contact your employer or contracting organisation.

Information we process

  • Account information: names, usernames, driver identifiers, organisation details and information needed for authentication.
  • Operational information: assigned tours, jobs, addresses, service details, work statuses, timestamps, comments and vehicle inspection records.
  • Work evidence: photographs, electronic signatures, barcodes, questionnaire responses and other information recorded during work activities.
  • Location information: GPS coordinates associated with work activities, including job updates, tour actions and vehicle checks.
  • Device and technical information: device and installation identifiers, App version, application errors, diagnostic records and information necessary to operate and support the App.

How your information is used

Your information is used to authenticate authorised users and provide access to assigned work; display and manage tours, jobs and vehicle inspections; record work activity and evidence; synchronise information with the customer's Business Central environment; and maintain, secure and support the App.

Location

The App uses location services to record GPS coordinates associated with relevant work activities, such as job updates, tour actions and vehicle inspections. Location information may be transmitted to Tegos-hosted services and synchronised with the customer's business systems.

In this release, location is accessed while the App is in use for relevant work activities. The App does not use continuous background location tracking. You can manage location permissions through your device settings; disabling location access may affect location-dependent features.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used to take work photos and scan supported barcodes or QR codes. The App does not request microphone access or record audio.

Where information is stored and who receives it

TegosSuite Driver uses Microsoft Azure to host services supporting the App, including the processing and synchronisation of operational information. Relevant information is exchanged between the App and Tegos-hosted services, and between those services and the customer's Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central environment. Authorised customer administrators, users and support personnel may access information according to their responsibilities.

Direct access to the Azure SQL database is restricted to authorised Tegos personnel. Customer drivers do not have direct database access; they access information through the App's APIs, which return data authorised for their account. Tour and job information is sent from the customer's Business Central environment to Tegos-hosted services; updates recorded in the App are sent back through those services.

  • Azure SQL: stores account, tour, job, vehicle-check and related operational information.
  • Azure Blob Storage: some API request files are temporarily stored for processing. Files are deleted after successful processing. If processing fails, a file may remain in storage until the issue is resolved or the file is otherwise removed.
  • Azure Application Insights: receives service diagnostics and operational telemetry.
  • Firebase: Firebase data collection, including Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Authentication and Messaging, is disabled for this release. The App does not send its data to those Firebase services in this release.
  • Google Maps: may receive a work address or coordinates if mapping is enabled for your organisation and you use that feature.
  • Expo Updates: provides App updates.

Tegos does not sell personal information. Your organisation may use additional connected systems; ask your employer or business customer which systems receive your work information.

International transfers

Operational data is transferred from the customer's Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central environment to Tegos-hosted Microsoft Azure services for processing and storage. Tegos's Azure SQL database and related services are hosted in the North Europe (Ireland) region. Customer drivers do not access the database directly; they access information authorised for their account through the App's APIs. Direct database access is restricted to authorised Tegos personnel. Any international transfer or access from another country is subject to applicable data protection requirements and safeguards.

Lawful basis

The customer organisation, as data controller, is responsible for determining and informing drivers of the lawful basis for processing operational information. Tegos processes that information on the customer's instructions as a data processor.

How long information is kept

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfil operational requirements and comply with applicable legal and contractual obligations.

We run a daily database cleanup process. Job records older than three months are deleted together with associated service records, barcodes, photographs and comments. Other information is retained according to its purpose and applicable legal and contractual requirements.

Application Insights diagnostic records are retained for up to 90 days, depending on the record type. Azure SQL automated recovery backups are retained for up to 35 days. Information held within a customer's Business Central environment is subject to that customer's own retention policies.

Information stored on your device

The App stores some information locally to support sign-in, offline work and synchronisation. This may include authentication tokens, a remembered username, work records, queued requests and diagnostic logs. Signing out removes some sign-in information but does not remove all locally stored work information or the remembered username. The App's “Clear Local” option deletes local App data and may remove work that has not synchronised.

Data security

Tegos uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration and disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and deletion requests

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information.

To request deletion of your driver account or personal information, contact the organisation that provided your driver account. That customer organisation may submit a deletion request to Tegos at any time by contacting info@tegos.eu. Tegos will process requests for information held in its systems, subject to applicable legal, contractual and operational retention requirements.

The organisation that provided your account is responsible for reviewing the request and coordinating with Tegos Systems Ltd where necessary.

Deleting or disabling an account or work record in Business Central does not automatically delete the corresponding information from Tegos's Azure SQL database; a separate deletion request to Tegos is required. Requests are assessed according to applicable data protection requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

For questions about personal information processed directly by Tegos Systems Ltd, contact info@tegos.eu.

The App does not provide a self-service account deletion control. For questions about the deletion process, contact your employer or contracting organisation. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection supervisory authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, our services or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be made available through the App or this webpage.

For questions about how your organisation uses your information, contact the data controller or your employer.
